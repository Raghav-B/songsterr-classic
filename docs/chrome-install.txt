Songsterr Classic — Chrome

1. Extract this ZIP somewhere you will keep it.
2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
3. Click Load unpacked and select the extracted extension folder.
4. Open a song on songsterr.com, then open Songsterr Classic from Extensions.
5. Turn the switch on. Turn it off to return to the current interface.

Keep the extracted folder: Chrome reads the extension from it.
Switching reloads the song tab. Save your work first.
Classic is enabled per tab and resets when the browser restarts.

Updating: replace the extracted files, then click Reload on the extension's
card at chrome://extensions. Save any open song edits before updating.

Removing: turn Classic off, then choose Remove at chrome://extensions.
Reload any remaining Classic tabs after removing the extension.

Saving and reopening edits has not yet been verified end to end. Test on a
disposable private song before relying on it for your work.
