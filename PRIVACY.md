# Privacy

Songsterr Classic does not operate a backend or add its own analytics. It runs
in your browser and changes how Songsterr's interface loads.

The extension uses access to Songsterr pages to load local frontend files and
adapt compatible requests. Its on/off setting is held in tab-scoped session
rules. It does not export cookies, passwords or browser history.

Songs, account requests, playback resources and any telemetry in Songsterr's
frontend still communicate with Songsterr and its services under their policies.
Classic is not a tracker blocker or an offline music library.

The GitHub and X icons open those sites only when clicked; those services have
their own privacy policies. Bug reports are voluntary—please do not include
credentials, private song data or personal browsing details.
